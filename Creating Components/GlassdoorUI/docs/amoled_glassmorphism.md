# AMOLED Glassmorphism Design Token Guide

- Substrates rely on pure pitch black (`#000000`) to guarantee zero backlight bleed on OLED panels.
- Elevated glass cards use 8%–14% white tint (`#FFFFFF`) with 1dp semi-translucent borders.
- Border stroke gradients simulate directional metallic lighting from upper-left to bottom-right.
- Provides deep tactile layer hierarchy without introducing visual noise or eye fatigue.
- Guarantees WCAG 2.2 AA text contrast by enforcing `#FFFFFF` and `#E0E0E0` typography over glass panels.
